📋 Group Guidelines & Rules

✅ Be Respectful

Maintain respect for all members. No offensive language, personal attacks, or harassment. Keep discussions friendly and constructive.

🚫 No Spam

Avoid posting irrelevant content, multiple forwards, or promotional material. Keep groups organized and focused on their purpose.

📸 Respect Privacy

Don't share others' photos or personal information without permission. Respect everyone's privacy and dignity.

🔕 Silent Hours

Avoid messaging between 11 PM and 7 AM. Use emergency groups for urgent matters. Respect everyone's sleep.

💬 On-Topic Discussion

Keep messages relevant to the group's purpose. Use specific groups for specific topics. Help organize and keep groups clean.

🙏 Admin's Authority

Respect admin decisions. Follow group rules and guidelines. Violations may lead to removal from the group.
