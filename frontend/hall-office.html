📋 Hall Office Overview

The Hall Office is the administrative hub of Bijoy 24 Hall, responsible for managing all hall operations, student services, disciplinary matters, and coordination with university administration. Our dedicated team works tirelessly to ensure a safe, welcoming, and conducive environment for all residents.

The office handles student admissions to the hall, hostel allocations, complaint resolutions, emergency support, and maintains communication between residents and the university administration. We are committed to providing professional and timely services to all our residents.
