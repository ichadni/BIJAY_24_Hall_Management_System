❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring for laundry?
You should bring your clothes and detergent. However, we provide detergent included in the slot price. You may bring your own detergent if you prefer a specific brand. Fabric softener is available at an additional cost.
Can I extend my washing slot?
Yes, you can extend your slot by paying ৳ 20 for each additional 15 minutes, subject to availability. Please request extension through the app or inform the laundry staff.
How do I cancel my booking?
You can cancel your booking up to 2 hours before your scheduled slot through the app. Full refund will be credited to your account. Late cancellations may incur a ৳ 10 cancellation fee.
Is there a monthly package available?
Yes! We offer unlimited monthly passes for ৳ 800. This gives you unlimited access to all machines for the entire month. Perfect for students who do laundry frequently.
What if a machine breaks down during my slot?
If a machine malfunctions, we'll provide an alternative machine of the same or higher capacity at no extra cost. If no alternatives are available, full refund will be issued.
How is payment processed?
Payment is deducted from your hall account or can be paid online via card/mobile banking during booking. Monthly passes must be purchased in advance.
